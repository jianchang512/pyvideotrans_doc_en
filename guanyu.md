## About Us

Welcome to the official documentation and tutorial site for pyvideotrans! We are the developer team behind the pyvideotrans open-source project, dedicated to providing an efficient and easy-to-use video translation tool. Our project began with a simple vision: to break language barriers and let video content cross borders, reaching a wider audience.

pyvideotrans is an open-source Python project (GitHub: https://github.com/jianchang512/pyvideotrans) focused on automated video translation and subtitle generation. Whether you're a content creator, an educator, or an individual looking to translate videos into multiple languages, we hope this tool saves you time and boosts your productivity. We believe the power of open-source lies in community collaboration and sharing — that's why we've fully open-sourced the code and welcome developers worldwide to contribute and help improve it.

At https://pyvideotrans.com, you'll find detailed documentation, usage tutorials, and the latest project updates. We strive to make every user feel at home, regardless of their technical background. Beyond providing the tool itself, we aim to build a platform for learning and exchange, where users can share experiences, offer suggestions, and collectively advance video translation technology.

Our team is made up of passionate and innovative developers, led by core developer jianchang512, as we explore smarter and more convenient solutions. In the future, we plan to introduce more features, such as AI-driven speech recognition and translation optimization, to meet growing user needs.

Thank you for your interest in and support of pyvideotrans! Whether you're using our tool or joining our development community, your participation drives us forward. Let's connect the world with technology!

### What is your monetization model?

This is a personal open-source project built on interest — there is no profit goal or business model. To extend the project's lifespan, we use several revenue methods to cover costs such as documentation server fees, Q&A site AI model API fees, and online channel API call fees during development.

- Small Google ads on the documentation site
- Voluntary donations via WeChat/Alipay QR codes / Ko-fi
- Monthly donations from users in the tech group
- Donations from GitHub users
