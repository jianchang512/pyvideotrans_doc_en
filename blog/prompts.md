## 1. Prompt Engineering? Sounds Fancy, But It's Actually Simple

Simply put, **prompt engineering** is about teaching you how to **speak clearly** so AI knows exactly what you want it to do.

For example, you want AI to write an article about the Spring Festival.
*   **Casual talk**: "Write something about the Spring Festival for me." — AI might give you a long, vague, and confusing article.
*   **Using prompt engineering**: "Help me write a roughly 200-word article introducing Spring Festival customs. It should be understandable for elementary school students, with a lively tone." — Now AI understands much better, and what it gives is more likely what you want.

**Another example**: AI is like a super-capable food delivery person. You have to tell them: "I want a spicy hot pot, mildly spicy, no cilantro, deliver to Building X, XX Community." The clearer you are, the more "on point" what they deliver will be. Prompt engineering is about helping you state your needs clearly so that the AI "delivery person" accurately delivers the "intellectual nourishment" you want.

**So why is it important?**

*   **Saves time and effort**: Being clear means AI gets it right the first time, saving you from back-and-forth revisions and boosting efficiency.
*   **More accurate results**: Clear instructions naturally lead to AI output that better matches your expectations, reducing "talking past each other."
*   **Beginner-friendly**: No need to understand complex technology or programming. As long as you can type and speak, you can use prompts to get AI to solve many problems for you.

## 2. What is a Prompt? It's the "Command" You Give to AI

A **prompt**, simply put, is the text you input to AI, telling it, "Hey, help me with this!"

It can be:

*   **A question**: For example, "What's the tallest building in Shanghai?"
*   **An instruction**: For example, "Write a thank-you letter to a client, about 100 words, tone should be formal."
*   **A request with a "template"**: For example, "Look at this well-written Taobao review: 'The product quality is super good, logistics is awesome, seller's service attitude is top-notch!' Help me imitate this style and write a review about the scarf I just bought."

**Let's compare (Chinese user scenarios):**

*   **Vague prompt**: "Talk about hot pot." (AI: Roger that! Here's a 5000-word report on the history, culture, and styles of hot pot... You: ???)
*   **Clear prompt**: "Use about 50 words to introduce the main characteristics of Chongqing hot pot, written for friends from other regions who have never tried it." (AI: Chongqing hot pot is famous for its numbing, spicy, fresh, and fragrant flavors. The beef tallow broth is its soul. Tripe and duck intestines are super fun to cook in it! Suitable for those who love strong flavors. You: Hmm, this is reliable!)

**Remember**: The more specific and clear your prompt is, the more satisfactory the answer AI gives you will be!

## 3. The Core of Prompt Engineering: Making AI Understand Your "Plain English"

Writing a good prompt is actually similar to giving a friend a task—it needs to be **simple, direct, and focused**. These three little tricks are essential for beginners:

### 3.1 Speak Clearly So AI Can Do the Right Job

AI can't read minds. You have to tell it plainly:

*   **What do you want to do?** (Write an article? Translate? Calculate something? Something else?)
*   **What format do you want?** (A paragraph? A list? A table?)
*   **Who is it for?** (For kids? For clients? For friends?)

**Example:**

*   **Scenario**: Just finished eating hot pot and want to show off on social media.
*   **Your prompt**: "Help me write a roughly 50-word social media post expressing my happy mood after just eating hot pot. The tone should be high-energy, suitable for friends to see."
*   **AI might write this**: "Wow! Just devoured a steaming hot spicy hot pot, the spice was perfect, and the meat was super tender! Laughed until our stomachs hurt with friends. Winter and hot pot are indeed the best match! So awesome! 😋 #DeserveToBeFat #HotPotSustainsLife"

### 3.2 Give AI an Example to "Follow the Pattern"

Worried AI might go off on a tangent with free rein? No problem, you can give it one or two examples first, just like a master craftsman teaching an apprentice—show them first. AI will learn from your example and produce results with a more consistent, more compliant style.

**Example:**

*   **Scenario**: Writing a Taobao positive review, wanting a consistent style.
*   **Your prompt**:
    ```
    Please imitate the format and style of the following review:

    【Example】
    Product: Sweater
    Review: This sweater feels super soft to the touch, and the color is very accurate, no color difference. Logistics was super fast, arrived in two days. The value for money is amazing!

    【Now it's your turn】
    Product: Scarf
    Review: ?
    ```
*   **AI might write this**: "This scarf is really super warm, the material is very comfortable, not itchy at all. The color is versatile, goes perfectly with my coat. The courier was also great, packaging intact. Love it!"

### 3.3 Don't Be Afraid to Try and Fail; Good Prompts Are "Refined"

AI isn't a deity either; it's perfectly normal not to be satisfied with the first result! Don't be discouraged, try adjusting your wording:

*   Try different words?
*   Add more descriptive details?
*   Change the tone requirement?

Try a few times, just like tuning a radio to find the signal. You'll eventually find the "best channel" that makes AI output what satisfies you most.

**Example:**

*   **First attempt**: "Write a poem about the moon."
    *   (The result might be too long, too profound, or the style might not be liked.)
*   **Optimized prompt**: "Write a four-line short poem describing the Mid-Autumn Festival moon. It should be simple and easy to understand, like a nursery rhyme, suitable for kindergarten children to listen to."
    *   **AI might write this**:
        ```
        The Mid-Autumn moon is round and round,
        Hanging in the sky like a jade plate.
        Children, come quickly and see,
        Eating mooncakes with happy faces.
        ```
    *   (Doesn't that feel much better?)

## 4. Common Prompt "Patterns" (Easier to Understand with Life Scenarios)

Depending on what you want to do, prompts also have several common "play styles." Let's illustrate with familiar examples from daily life:

### 4.1 Direct Ask / Direct Tell (Zero-shot Prompting)

The simplest and most direct way—just throw the question or task at AI without any preamble or explanation. Suitable for simple, straightforward tasks.

**Example:**

*   **Scenario**: Checking a date.
    *   **Prompt**: "What is the date and day of the week for the Dragon Boat Festival in 2025?"
    *   **AI output**: The Dragon Boat Festival in 2025 is on June 9th (Gregorian calendar), the fifth day of the fifth lunar month, Monday.
*   **Scenario**: No inspiration for dinner.
    *   **Prompt**: "Recommend a quick Cantonese dish suitable for making at home."
    *   **AI output**: You can try "Ginger Scallion Poached Chicken"—simple to make, the chicken is tender and smooth, with a rich ginger and scallion aroma, very appetizing. Or "Tomato and Egg Stir-fry," a classic home-cooked dish, nutritious and delicious.

**Suitable for**: Quickly looking up information, getting simple advice, impromptu Q&A, etc.

### 4.2 Give an Example for It to Learn (Few-shot Prompting)

First give AI one or two "templates" so it understands the format or style you want, then have it do the work. Suitable for tasks requiring specific output forms.

**Example:**

*   **Scenario**: Writing holiday greetings, wanting to maintain a consistent style.
    *   **Prompt**:
        ```
        Please write a greeting following the examples below:

        【Example 1】
        Recipient: Xiao Ming
        Occasion: Birthday
        Greeting: Xiao Ming, happy birthday! In the new year, may you be happy every day and make progress in your studies!

        【Example 2】
        Recipient: Xiao Hong
        Occasion: New Year
        Greeting: Xiao Hong, happy New Year! Wishing you good health, smooth work, and all the best!

        【Now it's your turn】
        Recipient: Xiao Gang
        Occasion: Mid-Autumn Festival
        Greeting: ?
        ```
    *   **AI output**: Xiao Gang, happy Mid-Autumn Festival! Wishing your family reunion, happiness, and mooncakes sweet to the heart!

**Suitable for**: Writing requiring fixed formats, specific styles, like weekly reports, reviews, various greetings, etc.

### 4.3 Let AI "Think Step by Step" (Chain-of-Thought Prompting)

For slightly more complex problems, like calculating a bill or analyzing a cause, you can guide AI to think and reason step by step instead of giving the answer directly. This usually leads to more accurate results.

**Example:**

*   **Scenario**: Calculating how much was spent shopping.
    *   **Prompt**: "I bought 2 jin of apples today, 12 yuan per jin; also bought 3 jin of oranges, 10 yuan per jin. Help me calculate how much I spent in total? Please write out the calculation steps."
    *   **AI output**:
        ```
        Okay, let's calculate step by step:
        1.  How much did the apples cost? 2 jin × 12 yuan/jin = 24 yuan.
        2.  How much did the oranges cost? 3 jin × 10 yuan/jin = 30 yuan.
        3.  How much was spent in total? 24 yuan + 30 yuan = 54 yuan.
        So, you spent a total of 54 yuan.
        ```

**Suitable for**: Mathematical calculations, logical reasoning, problem analysis, tasks requiring detailed explanation of the process.

## 6. Five Little Tricks to "Greatly Enhance" Your Prompt Skills

Want AI to understand you better and become your capable assistant? Try these five simple and practical little tricks—even beginners can master them easily!

### 6.1 Be Goal-Oriented, No Nonsense

Just like ordering food, directly say you want "Kung Pao Chicken," don't say "随便来个下饭的" (just give me something that goes well with rice).
*   **Not so good**: "Write something for me." (AI: Write what? A poem? A novel? Code?)
*   **Better this way**: "Help me write an about 80-word thank-you email to thank Manager Wang for the help he gave me in the meeting yesterday afternoon. The tone should be sincere and professional."
*   **AI might write this**: "Dear Manager Wang: Thank you very much for the valuable advice and support you gave me during the project meeting yesterday afternoon; it was very enlightening. Once again, my sincere thanks! Looking forward to more opportunities to learn from you in the future. Best regards! Xiao Li"

### 6.2 Provide Enough "Background Information," So AI Doesn't Stray

Tell AI some context, relevant circumstances, so it can better understand your needs.
*   **Scenario**: Wanting to praise a colleague in the company group chat.
*   **Prompt**: "Help me write a roughly 50-word praise message to post in the company's large group. The main content is to thank colleague Xiao Zhang for staying up late with me last night to solve that urgent customer complaint; his performance was特别给力. The tone should be sincere, with a bit of team spirit."
*   **AI output**: "Must give Xiao Zhang a big thumbs up! 👍 Last night's urgent customer matter, thanks to him staying up late together to handle it, professional and reliable! With such a capable comrade-in-arms, there's nothing our team can't handle! Thank you, Xiao Zhang! @Xiao Zhang"

### 6.3 Imitate "Examples" for Doubled Effect

Show AI a "template," and it can better imitate the style or format you want.
*   **Scenario**: Wanting to write a couplet about reading.
*   **Prompt**:
    ```
    Please write a couplet imitating the example below:

    【Example】
    Theme: Spring Festival
    Couplet: Firecrackers bid farewell to the old year, plum blossoms welcome the new spring.

    【Now please write】

### 6.4 Reject "Vagueness," Specify "Details"

The more specific you are, the better AI knows what to do.
*   **Not so good**: "Write me an ad." (AI: Ad for what product? For whom to see? What style?)
*   **Better this way**: "Write a roughly 30-word slogan for my pour-over coffee shop. Highlight 'freshly ground' and 'suitable for morning refreshment,' attracting nearby office workers."
*   **AI output**: "A cup of freshly ground good coffee in the morning, awakening your work inspiration! XX Coffee Shop, using fresh aroma, starts your energetic day! ☀️ #OfficeWorkerLifeline"

Don't expect AI to hit the mark perfectly on the first try. Try several times, like polishing jade, adjust your prompt, and the results will get better and better.

## 7. Fine-Tuning AI Output: "Temperature" and "Length"

Besides carefully crafting prompts, you can also influence the style and length of AI's responses by adjusting two small "switches." Beginners just need to know these two:

### 7.1 Temperature

*   **What is this?**: Can be understood as controlling the "creativity" or "divergence" of AI's answers. The value is usually between 0 and 1.
    *   **Low temperature (e.g., 0.1 - 0.3)**: AI answers will be more **conservative, rigorous**, tending towards facts and the most common statements. Suitable for scenarios requiring **accurate information**, like answering factual questions, making summaries.
    *   **High temperature (e.g., 0.7 - 1.0)**: AI answers will be more **creative, random**, possibly coming up with unexpected ideas. Suitable for scenarios requiring **inspiration,趣味性**, like writing stories, brainstorming ideas.
*   **How to use it?** (Different AI tools may have different setting methods, usually found in settings.)
    *   **Example 1: Low temperature (set to 0.2)**
        *   **Prompt**: "What are some must-visit parks in Beijing?"
        *   **AI output** (likely more standard): The Summer Palace, Temple of Heaven Park, Beihai Park.
    *   **Example 2: High temperature (set to 0.9)**
        *   **Prompt**: "Describe Beijing's summer in an exaggerated, funny tone."
        *   **AI output** (likely more放飞自我): Beijing's summer? It's practically a walking sauna, going out comes with a五花肉 special effect, no need to sprinkle cumin! The sun is so enthusiastic it's like wanting to become sworn brothers with you! The air conditioner is the lifesaver! 🤣

### 7.2 Output Length (Max Length / Max Tokens)

*   **What is this?**: As the name suggests, it controls the length of AI's response content.
*   **How to use it?**
    *   **Method 1 (Recommended)**: State it directly in the prompt, e.g., "**Use about 50 words** to introduce...", "**Write a paragraph not exceeding 100 words** about..."
    *   **Method 2**: In the AI tool's settings, find an option like "Maximum output length" or "Max Tokens" to set a limit. (Token can be roughly understood as the unit of text AI processes; one Chinese character might count as 1-2 tokens.)
*   **Example**:
    *   **Prompt**: "Summarize the main characteristics of pandas in one sentence."
    *   **AI output** (naturally very short): Pandas are known for their black-and-white fur, round and chubby bodies, and love for eating bamboo.

**Beginner's advice**:

*   Want AI to **answer questions properly**? **Lower the temperature** (e.g., 0.2).
*   Want AI to **think outside the box and give some surprises**? **Raise the temperature** (e.g., 0.8).
*   Want **how long an answer**? Best to **state it directly in the prompt**, e.g., "Write me a 300-word..."

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs for Beginners!)

**Q1: I don't know any technology at all. Can I really learn to use prompt engineering?**
A: **Absolutely!** The core of prompt engineering is "speaking clearly." Think of it as communicating with a very smart but somewhat "single-minded" assistant. You just need to use our usual way of speaking to clearly assign tasks, like asking it to write an email, answer a question, or brainstorm an idea—no programming knowledge needed at all. The examples in this guide are all in plain English—just follow along!

**Q2: I'm not satisfied with AI's answer. What should I do? Am I too dumb?**
A: **Of course not!** AI isn't omnipotent either; sometimes it确实会“犯傻”. At such times, definitely don't get discouraged. Trying several times is normal:
    *   **Add some ingredients**: Try adding some constraints to the prompt, like "**write it for my mom to read**", "**use simpler words**", "**must include XX keywords**".
    *   **Give a template**: Refer to the "Few-shot Prompting" mentioned earlier—give it an example to learn from.
    *   **Ask differently**: Ask the same meaning from a different angle. For example, instead of saying "write a story," try "help me outline a short story about friendship, including the beginning, middle, and end, about 150 words."

**Q3: Is there any "universal formula" or template I can use?**
A: While there's no absolute "universal formula," this **thinking framework** can help you get started:
    **【The role you want AI to play (optional)】+【The task you want AI to complete】+【Key requirements/constraints (e.g., format, length, tone, style, audience, must include/exclude what)】+【Provide examples (optional)】**

    *   **Example 1 (writing copy)**: "**[Role: Senior advertising copywriter]** **[Task: Write a roughly 100-word slogan]** **[Requirements: Promote the newly launched smartwatch, highlight health monitoring features and long battery life, tone should be tech-savvy, attract young people]**"
    *   **Example 2 (translation)**: "**[Task: Translate 'Eat a good breakfast' into idiomatic American English]** **[Requirements: Tone should be colloquial, like a reminder between friends]**"
    *   **Example 3 (explaining a concept)**: "**[Role: Patient teacher]** **[Task: Explain what 'cloud computing' is in simple language]** **[Requirements: Use an analogy so someone with no technical knowledge can understand, under 150 words]**"
