## Batch Extract Audio from Videos

If you have a large number of videos and want to extract the audio from them into audio files, you can use this feature.

![](https://pvtr2.pyvideotrans.com/images/getaudio1.jpg)

### How to Use

1. Select video file(s) in the software interface. You can choose one or multiple files.

2. Click "Start Execution". After completion, click "Open Result Directory".
