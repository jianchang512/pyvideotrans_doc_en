## Audition Voiceover

1.  Select the target language, then choose a voiceover character name. A button labeled `Audition Voiceover` will appear above the voice character list. Click it to start the audition.

2.  During the audition, audio will be generated in real-time based on the text `你好啊，我亲爱的朋友，希望你的每一天都是美好愉快的` `Hello, my dear friend. I hope your every day is beautiful and enjoyable`. It may take a few seconds to start playing, please wait patiently.
