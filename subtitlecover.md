## Subtitle Format Conversion

Used for batch converting subtitle files from one format to another, for example, converting srt subtitles to ass or vtt subtitles. Supports batch conversion.

![image-20240901132956536](https://pvtr2.pyvideotrans.com/images/image-20240901132956536.png)

## Audio and Video Format Conversion

Used for batch converting audio or video files to other formats, for example, converting wav audio to mp3.

![image-20240901132931303](https://pvtr2.pyvideotrans.com/images/image-20240901132931303.png)


## Batch Extract Audio from Video

If you have a large number of videos and want to extract the audio from them into separate audio files, you can use this feature.

![](https://pvtr2.pyvideotrans.com/images/getaudio1.jpg)

**How to Use**

1.  Select video files on the software interface. You can choose one or multiple files.

2.  Click "Start Execution". After completion, click "Open Result Directory".
